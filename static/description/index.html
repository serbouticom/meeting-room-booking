Meeting Room Booking

A shared room calendar with conflict detection, approval workflow and recurring bookings

Key features

 Conflict detection

Two bookings that overlap on the same room can never both be confirmed. Draft requests never block a slot, so users can still prepare a request while another one is pending.

 Approval workflow

Draft → To Approve → Confirmed (or Rejected / Cancelled). Flag a room as Requires Approval and every request is routed to its responsible user through a scheduled activity.

 Capacity & equipment

Describe each room with a seat capacity and the equipment it offers (projector, video conference, whiteboard…). Organizers get a warning when the expected headcount exceeds the room capacity.

 Recurring bookings

Repeat a booking every N days, weeks or months until a chosen date. Every occurrence is created up front and linked to the original booking so you can review the whole series at a glance.

How it works

  1. A user opens Meeting Rooms › Bookings, checks the calendar and creates a request.
  2. They click Submit. If the room needs approval the booking goes to To Approve and the room's responsible gets an activity; otherwise it is Confirmed immediately.
  3. The responsible (or a Meeting Room Manager) clicks Approve or Reject.
  4. Confirmed and to-approve bookings reserve the slot: any overlapping request is refused.

Configuration

Everything lives under the Meeting Rooms app. Managers configure rooms and equipment from Meeting Rooms › Configuration.

Access rights

  • User – sees every booking (availability), manages their own, approves bookings for rooms they are responsible for.
  • Manager – full control over rooms, equipment and all bookings.

Compatibility

Odoo 17.0 – Community & Enterprise. Only depends on mail.
