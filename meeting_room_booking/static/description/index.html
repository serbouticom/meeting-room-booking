A closer look

How it works day to day

📅
FEATURE 1

See availability at a glance

Every booking lives on one calendar, grouped and colored by room. Check a slot before you ask for it.

  • ✓Overlapping requests on the same room are automatically refused
  • ✓Drafts never block a slot; only To Approve and Confirmed bookings do
  • ✓Filter by room, organizer or status, or jump to My Bookings and Upcoming
✅
FEATURE 2

Approvals, only where you want them

Flag a room as Requires Approval and its bookings follow a controlled path. Other rooms confirm instantly.

  • ✓Draft → To Approve → Confirmed (or Rejected / Cancelled)
  • ✓The room's responsible receives a scheduled activity as soon as a request is submitted
  • ✓Only that responsible or a Meeting Room Manager can approve or reject
👥
FEATURE 3

Describe rooms once, reuse everywhere

Give each room a capacity, a location, a responsible and the equipment it offers.

  • ✓Seat capacity with an instant warning when the headcount is too high
  • ✓Reusable equipment tags: projector, video conference, whiteboard…
  • ✓Color index to tell rooms apart on the calendar
🔁
FEATURE 4

Book once, repeat automatically

Set up a recurring series in seconds instead of booking every week by hand.

  • ✓Repeat every N days, weeks or months until a chosen date
  • ✓All occurrences are generated up front and linked to the original booking
  • ✓Each occurrence is independent: cancel or reschedule one without touching the rest
🔐
FEATURE 5

Simple, safe access rights

Two dedicated groups keep permissions clear, on top of standard multi-company rules.

  • ✓User: sees every booking to check availability, manages their own, approves rooms they are responsible for
  • ✓Manager: full control over rooms, equipment and every booking
  • ✓Rooms and bookings are scoped per company out of the box
Workflow

From request to confirmed room in three steps

1
Request
Pick a room and a time slot on the calendar, then click Submit.
2
Approve
The responsible gets an activity and approves or rejects.
3
Confirmed
The slot is reserved. Any overlapping request is refused.

FAQ

Questions you may have

Does it work on Odoo Online?
Yes. It only depends on mail and installs the same way on Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or on-premise.
What happens if two people book the same room at the same time?
The second request is refused with a clear message naming the conflicting booking.
Can I require approval for some rooms only?
Yes. Requires Approval is a checkbox on each room. Other rooms confirm instantly.
Can I cancel a single occurrence of a recurring booking?
Yes. Every occurrence is its own booking linked to the series: cancel or reschedule one without affecting the others.
Does it support multiple companies?
Yes. Rooms and bookings carry a company and follow standard Odoo multi-company rules.
Does it replace the Calendar or Appointments apps?
No. It is a focused standalone app for internal room booking and does not touch calendar.event.
Can I try it first?
Yes. Install it on a test database: demo data ships with sample rooms and equipment.
