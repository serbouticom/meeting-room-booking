A closer look

Shared calendar & conflict detection

Every booking lives on one calendar, grouped and colored by room, so anyone can check availability before requesting a slot.

  • Overlapping requests on the same room are automatically refused.
  • Only To Approve and Confirmed bookings occupy a slot — drafts never block a colleague from planning around them.
  • Filter and group by room, organizer or status; jump straight to My Bookings or Upcoming.

Approval workflow

Not every room should confirm itself. Flag a room as Requires Approval and its bookings follow a controlled path:

  • Draft → To Approve → Confirmed (or Rejected / Cancelled).
  • The room's Responsible gets a scheduled activity the moment a request is submitted.
  • Only that responsible — or a Meeting Room Manager — can approve or reject; everyone else just sees the button disappear.

Capacity & equipment

Describe each room once, then let the app do the checking:

  • Set a seat capacity and location for every room.
  • Tag rooms with reusable equipment — projector, video conference, whiteboard…
  • Organizers see an instant warning when the expected headcount exceeds the room capacity.

Recurring bookings

Set up a recurring series once instead of booking every week by hand:

  • Repeat every N days, weeks or months, until a chosen end date.
  • All occurrences are generated up front and linked to the original booking.
  • Each occurrence remains an independent booking — cancel or reschedule one without touching the rest of the series.

Security & multi-company

Two dedicated access groups keep things simple:

  • User – sees every booking to check availability, manages their own, and approves bookings for rooms they are responsible for.
  • Manager – full control over rooms, equipment and every booking.
  • Rooms and bookings are scoped per company out of the box.

How it works

  1. A user opens Meeting Rooms › Bookings, checks the calendar and creates a request for a room, date and time.
  2. They click Submit. If the room needs approval the booking moves to To Approve and the responsible is notified; otherwise it is Confirmed right away.
  3. The responsible (or a Meeting Room Manager) clicks Approve or Reject.
  4. Confirmed and to-approve bookings reserve the slot: any overlapping request on the same room is refused.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work on Odoo Online?
Yes. The module only depends on mail and installs the same way on Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or an on-premise server.
What happens if two people book the same room at the same time?
The second request is refused with a clear error naming the conflicting booking, as soon as it is submitted or confirmed.
Can I require approval for some rooms only?
Yes, Requires Approval is a checkbox on each room. Rooms without it confirm bookings instantly.
Can I cancel a single occurrence of a recurring booking?
Yes, every occurrence is its own booking record linked to the series — cancel, reschedule or reject one without affecting the others.
Does it support multiple companies?
Yes, rooms and bookings carry a company and are scoped with standard Odoo multi-company rules.
Does it replace the standard Odoo Calendar/Appointments apps?
No, it is a focused, standalone app dedicated to internal room booking; it does not touch calendar.event or the Appointments app.
Can I try it before buying?
Install it on a test database first — demo data ships with two sample rooms and equipment so you can explore every workflow immediately.
